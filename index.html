Gabriel Rourke

PhD Candidate in Economics, Boston University

I study how employer consolidation affects wages and employment, with a focus on health care labor markets.

My work is in labor economics, health economics, and industrial organization. My current project examines how hospital mergers and acquisitions have changed employment and pay for nurses and other clinical staff across U.S. metropolitan areas.

I am currently the instructor for ECON 390: Labor Market Economics at the University of Massachusetts Boston, and I co-chaired the BU Graduate Economics Association in 2025–2026.

I have also worked as a research assistant at the London School of Economics and the University of Chicago, and as a research associate at RPB Energy Economics. I hold an MA in Economics from Boston University and a BS in Mathematics and BA in Economics from the University of Chicago.
